Foundations

Permitted Obstructions is a photographic and research-based book exploring ping-pong as a form of social space in New York City.

The project will attempt to establish the space that exists between organized sport and informal play. Established within designated tables and courts, to improvised games that interrupt predetermined structures and established everyday movement. By shifting where and how ping-pong happens, the project asks how play can interrupt, redefine, and create public space through user interaction.
